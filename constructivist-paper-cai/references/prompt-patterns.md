# Prompt Patterns

Replace bracketed content with task-specific information. Keep supplied text quoted exactly.

## Poster or campaign visual

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: [aspect ratio and exact delivery size] poster or campaign visual
Primary request: Create an original visual for [subject/title] using the established Constructivist paper sci-fi collage direction.
Concept: [premise] triggers or reveals [event, tension, or consequence].
Composition: strong diagonal axis; asymmetrical wedges; circular or triangular montage windows; dramatic scale contrast; purposeful dark negative space.
Title text (verbatim): "[exact title]" exactly once. Use readable Constructivist/Cubist display lettering assembled from interlocking geometric paper planes.
Secondary text (verbatim): "[exact secondary text]" only if required.
Palette and materials: deep navy fibrous paper, bone-white collage, desaturated teal technical geometry, dusty purple shadows, one rusty-red central event, tiny burnt-orange signals.
Constraints: safe margins; clear hierarchy; no extra text; do not copy a specific historical poster or living artist.
Avoid: static centered portrait, symmetrical layout, bright red takeover, glossy rendering, unreadable title.
```

## Cover, thumbnail, or icon

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: [cover, thumbnail, or icon] readable at [target viewing size]
Primary request: Create [one subject and one action or idea] in the Constructivist paper sci-fi collage system.
Subject: One dominant faceless angular paper-cut figure or object, identified by [silhouette cues].
Composition: one subject, one action or idea, one dominant rusty-red impact symbol; bold centered or intentionally off-center silhouette; minimal background geometry.
Palette: deep navy-black, warm bone-white, desaturated teal, dusty purple, rusty vermilion, tiny burnt orange.
Materials: fibrous construction paper, angular facets, torn edges, dry ink, photocopy grain, subtle layer shadows.
Text (verbatim): "[exact short text]" only.
Constraints: clear at target size; compose for the final shape; no extra text.
Avoid: glossy 3D, Pop Art dots, neon cyberpunk, cute cartoon, realistic portrait.
```

## Editorial or narrative illustration

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: [editorial or narrative illustration and delivery size]
Subject: [idea, event, location, or argument].
Figures and objects: angular paper-cut forms with identity carried by silhouette, posture, clothing, and props; faces absent or highly reduced.
Framing: cinematic crop or multi-panel archival montage; use negative space and one directional light or impact cue.
Visual logic: connect [premise] to [consequence] through paths, windows, scale, or orbital geometry.
Palette and material: follow the core style guide.
Text: no text unless explicitly supplied.
Avoid: conventional anime staging, realistic faces, smooth gradients, excessive interface overlay.
```

## Interface or information graphic

```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: [interface, diagram, or information graphic]
Primary request: Design a usable dark scientific-instrument visual system in the Constructivist paper sci-fi direction.
Structure: deep navy panels, cold-white rules, broken circular scans, grids, nodes, and restrained geometric wedges.
States: rusty red for errors and danger; desaturated teal for stable states; burnt orange for one high-priority cue.
Typography: readable narrow sans-serif or monospaced technical text; no decorative pseudo-code.
Materials: subtle paper grain and print misregistration without compromising legibility.
Constraints: comprehension and accessibility first; consistent spacing; clear hierarchy; represent real supplied information accurately.
```
